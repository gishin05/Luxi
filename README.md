# Luxi

### Full-featured reader & player
Discover and enjoy manga, anime, webtoons, comics, and more on your Android device.

## Features

<div align="left">

* Local reading and playback of content.
* Configurable reader and built-in video player.
* Tracker support: [MangaBaka](https://mangabaka.org), [MyAnimeList](https://myanimelist.net/), [AniList](https://anilist.co/), [Kitsu](https://kitsu.app/), [MangaUpdates](https://mangaupdates.com), [Shikimori](https://shikimori.one), [Bangumi](https://bgm.tv/), and [Hikka](https://hikka.io/) support.
* Categories to organize your library.
* Light and dark themes.
* Schedule updating your library for new chapters and episodes.
* Create backups locally or restore across devices.
